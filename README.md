# # DuPont v. Kolon Industries – Digital Forensics Case Study

## Overview
This repository contains a digital forensic investigation report on the **DuPont v. Kolon Industries case (Case ID: IE-2009-01)**, focusing on the theft of trade secrets related to DuPont’s **Kevlar and Heracron technologies**.  

The report demonstrates how forensic methodologies (data acquisition, preservation, and analysis) can uncover insider threats, unauthorized data transfers, and suspicious email activity.

---

## Repository Contents
- **Report Document** – Full case study with analysis, findings, and recommendations.  
- **Figures & Tables** – Screenshots and structured evidence logs.  
- **References** – Tools and sources used in the investigation.  

---

## Tools & Methodologies
- **FTK Imager** – Forensic imaging of USB drives, integrity verification with MD5/SHA1.  
- **MXToolbox Header Analyzer** – Email header validation (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).  
- **Chain of Custody Procedures** – Ensuring admissibility of evidence in litigation.  

---

##  Key Findings
- Suspicious folder `items_company` showing rapid batch transfers of sensitive files.  
- Email titled *“Secret info of DuPont”* with technically valid headers but highly suspicious context.  
- Evidence of insider involvement and potential concealment attempts.  

---

## Recommendations
- Restrict access to sensitive files.  
- Block unauthorized USB usage.  
- Monitor file transfers and email activity.  
- Train employees on data security.  
- Strengthen forensic readiness and incident response plans.
